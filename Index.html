Passport Full of Plans

where I've been, and where I'm going next

Traveling is the one thing that makes me feel like I'm learning something every single day. Living in Colorado is great, but there's something about landing in a city where I can't read the signs yet that I really like. I love figuring out how transit works, eating from a random little food stall , and just walking until I'm a little lost. My big goal is to find a country that I want to use as a home base and travel out to other countries from there. So this page is basically my running list of what I love about travel and where I want to go.

Left: Limon, Costa Rica. Right: Medellín, Colombia. Both are high on my list.

Things I Enjoy Doing When I Travel

  • Eating at markets and trying whatever looks good
  • Walking a new city with no real plan for the day
  • Visiting museums and nearby small towns
  • Finding a good coffee shop to sit in and write a little
  • Guided tours for extra context on the area that I woulnd't have known

My Top 5 Travel Destinations

  1. Limon, Costa Rica
  2. Medellín, Colombia
  3. Manila, Philippines
  4. Hoi An, Vietnam
  5. Cusco, Peru

City Comparison

City Country Best season to visit Famous for
Limón Costa Rica September to October Caribbean beaches, Afro-Caribbean food and culture, and nearby Cahuita and Tortuguero parks
Medellín Colombia December to March "City of Eternal Spring" weather and its cable car system
Manila Philippines December to April History, street food, and island-hopping trips
